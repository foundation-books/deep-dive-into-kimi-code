# Public metric definitions used by this teaching packet

- Balanced accuracy is the mean recall across classes. This packet uses the
  unadjusted, unweighted single-label case. Source: scikit-learn 1.9.1,
  `balanced_accuracy_score` documentation,
  https://scikit-learn.org/1.9/modules/generated/sklearn.metrics.balanced_accuracy_score.html
- Ordinary normalized accuracy is the fraction of predictions that are
  correct. Source: scikit-learn 1.9.1, `accuracy_score` documentation,
  https://scikit-learn.org/1.9/modules/generated/sklearn.metrics.accuracy_score.html

These definitions are public source material. `counts.csv`, the methodology,
and the defective program are book-authored teaching fixtures, not published
classifier measurements.
